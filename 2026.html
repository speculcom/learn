四个词，补上四条最常被跳过的线Four terms for four lines that are usually skipped

每个词条都写出处、边界与「它没主张什么」；读完能判断一个产品页说的是哪一层，而不是多背一个缩写。Each entry gives sources, boundaries and what it does not claim; after reading, you can tell which layer a product page is talking about instead of memorising another acronym.

01训练Training

GRPOGRPO →

用同一道题的一组回答互相比较，省掉 PPO 的 critic。看懂「推理能力是训出来的」到底在训什么。Compare a group of answers to one question instead of training a PPO critic. Understand what “reasoning is trained” actually trains.

边界Boundary

不是无需奖励模型；组大小与奖励设计直接影响稳定性。Not reward-model-free; group size and reward design drive stability.

02推理Inference

MLAMLA →

把 KV 缓存压成低秩潜向量，长上下文的第一道墙因此松动。Compresses KV cache into a low-rank latent, relaxing the first wall in long context.

边界Boundary

省的是显存与带宽，不是 FLOPs；不是无限上下文。Saves memory and bandwidth, not FLOPs; not infinite context.

03表征Representation

JEPAJEPA →

在表示空间预测被遮住的部分，而不是重建像素或 token；2026 的 JEPA-PRH 用它检验跨模态对齐。Predicts masked parts in representation space rather than reconstructing pixels or tokens; JEPA-PRH uses it to test cross-modal alignment in 2026.

边界Boundary

对齐分数不是生成质量；预印本规模有限。Alignment is not generation quality; the preprint is limited in scale.

04生成Generation

Flow MatchingFlow Matching →

学一个从噪声到数据的速度场，用 ODE 积分采样；少步生成的主流底层之一。Learns a velocity field from noise to data and samples by ODE integration; one mainstream basis for few-step generation.

边界Boundary

不是一步生成；步数与蒸馏有关，FID 不等于人类偏好。Not one-step generation; steps depend on distillation, and FID is not human preference.
