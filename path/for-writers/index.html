内容工作者路线The Writer's Route

约 40 分钟about 40 minutes

给靠文字吃饭的人：先懂 AI 怎么生成、哪里会错，再学会让它替你干活而不是替你编活。For people who make a living on words: first understand how AI generates and where it goes wrong, then make it work for you instead of inventing work for you.

按这个顺序读Read in this order
  1. 生成式 AIGenerative AI
  2. 提示词Prompt
  3. 上下文窗口Context Window
  4. 幻觉Hallucination
  5. 幻觉的成因Why Hallucination Happens
  6. 知识截止Knowledge Cutoff
  7. 联网 / 检索增强Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RAG
  8. 温度Temperature
  9. 系统提示System Prompt
  10. 水印与内容凭证Watermarks and Content Credentials
学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 说出幻觉的两种成因，各配一个写作场景里的例子
  2. 用「联网检索 + 自己核对」替代直接采信 AI 给的引用
  3. 拿到一段 AI 文字，说清哪些部分必须人工复核、复核什么
  1. Name two causes of hallucination, each with an example from a writing scenario
  2. Replace trusting AI-provided citations on faith with retrieval plus your own verification
  3. Given a passage of AI text, say which parts require human review and what to check
