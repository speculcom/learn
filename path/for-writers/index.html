内容工作者路线The Writer's Route

约 40 分钟about 40 minutes

围绕资料整理、写作与引用，了解生成限制和核对方法。读完后用一小段真实资料练习，而非只记提问技巧。Study limitations and checks for research, writing and citations. Then practice on a short source passage rather than memorizing prompting tips.

按这个顺序读Read in this order
  1. 生成式 AIGenerative AI
  2. 提示词Prompt
  3. 上下文窗口Context Window
  4. 幻觉Hallucination
  5. 幻觉的成因Why Hallucination Happens
  6. 知识截止Knowledge Cutoff
  7. 联网 / 检索增强Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RAG
  8. 温度Temperature
  9. 系统提示System Prompt
  10. 水印与内容凭证Watermarks and Content Credentials
学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 说出幻觉的两种成因，各配一个写作场景里的例子
  2. 用「联网检索 + 自己核对」替代直接采信 AI 给的引用
  3. 拿到一段 AI 文字，说清哪些部分必须人工复核、复核什么
  1. Name two causes of hallucination, each with an example from a writing scenario
  2. Replace trusting AI-provided citations on faith with retrieval plus your own verification
  3. Given a passage of AI text, say which parts require human review and what to check

先试着回答，再展开参考答案。答不上来的地方，可从上方步骤返回对应词条。参考回答是核对要点，不要求逐字复述。Try answering before opening the reference. Revisit the relevant step above if needed. These answers highlight key ideas; you do not need to repeat the wording.

第 1 题 · 参考回答Question 1 · Reference answer

输出会受资料缺失、歧义和生成随机性影响。例：让模型编写活动介绍时，提供已确认的时间地点，并要求未提供的信息标为未知。Missing sources, ambiguity and generation randomness can affect output. For an event notice, provide confirmed time and venue and require missing facts to remain unknown.

第 2 题 · 参考回答Question 2 · Reference answer

打开原始页面核对作者、日期与原文，再确认它是否支持你的句子；能打开链接并不代表引文成立。无法核实的引用不要当证据使用。Open the original page to check author, date and wording, then confirm that it supports the claim. A reachable URL is not enough. Do not treat unverified citations as evidence.

第 3 题 · 参考回答Question 3 · Reference answer

复核姓名、数字、引用、事实判断和对外承诺；涉及专业判断时另找可靠依据。编辑还应检查文字是否改变了原意，而不只修语法。Verify names, numbers, quotations, factual claims and external commitments. Seek reliable support for specialist judgments. Check preservation of meaning, not grammar alone.
