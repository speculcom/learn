深入Deep dive原理、评测与安全审查Methods, evaluation and safety review

一周one week

六段各选代表主题，共二十五步。把能力、成本、许可与评测放在一起看，最后用具体任务检查自己的选择。Follow twenty-five representative steps across six stages. Connect capability, cost, licensing and evaluation, then examine a choice for a concrete task.

学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 拿到一个新模型的介绍页，能列出该问的问题：能力、边界、成本、许可、锁定
  2. 判断一件事该用 API 还是本地跑，并说出判断依据
  3. 看懂一份基准榜单，并说出它至少一处代表不了你的实际效果
  4. 为什么第一周先跑通一条最小链路，而不是先读完全部理论？
  5. 每日复盘应该记录什么，才能让第二周继续？
  6. 怎么判断一个周计划已经过载？
  7. 项目从 demo 到可交付，最先补哪三样？
  8. 为什么同一周内不要同时换模型、数据和评测？
  9. 周结束时，什么证据能证明你真的学会了？
  10. 第二周该保留什么、删掉什么？
  1. Given a new model's page, list the questions to ask: capability, limits, cost, licence, lock-in
  2. Decide API or local for a given job, and state the reasoning
  3. Read a benchmark table and name at least one thing it cannot say about your actual use
  4. Why build one minimal pipeline in week one instead of reading all the theory first?
  5. What should a daily review record so week two can continue?
  6. How do you know a one-week plan is overloaded?
  7. Moving from demo to delivery, what three things come first?
  8. Why not change model, data and evaluation in the same week?
  9. At the end of the week, what evidence shows you actually learned?
  10. In week two, what should you keep and what should you cut?

先试着回答，再展开参考答案。答不上来的地方，可从上方步骤返回对应词条。参考回答是核对要点，不要求逐字复述。Try answering before opening the reference. Revisit the relevant step above if needed. These answers highlight key ideas; you do not need to repeat the wording.

第 1 题 · 参考回答Question 1 · Reference answer

先写任务和验收条件，再查能力限制、数据去向、总成本、许可证、导出与迁移方式。不能用一个榜单名次替代这些问题。Define the task and acceptance criteria, then check limits, data handling, total cost, licensing, export and migration. A leaderboard position cannot answer these questions.

第 2 题 · 参考回答Question 2 · Reference answer

示例：低频且无部署经验的任务可先评估 API；不能把数据交给外部服务时，应评估符合约束的本地或私有方案。两边都要核算成本、质量、维护和硬件限制。For example, assess an API for occasional work without deployment expertise; assess a suitable local/private option when external data transfer is disallowed. Compare cost, quality, maintenance and hardware limits in both cases.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 3 题 · 参考回答Question 3 · Reference answer

核对基准版本、输入、工具与评分口径；它未必覆盖你的中文材料、长文档、响应时间或失败成本。用自己的代表性任务补足缺口。Check benchmark version, inputs, tools and scoring. It may omit your Chinese documents, long inputs, latency requirements or cost of failure. Add representative tasks of your own.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 4 题 · 参考回答Question 4 · Reference answer

最小链路会暴露真实约束：数据、接口、成本、延迟和验收。理论可以按问题补，先读完全部理论容易把时间花在不会用到的章节上。A minimal pipeline exposes real constraints: data, interfaces, cost, latency and acceptance. Read theory as questions arise; reading everything first spends time on chapters you may never use.

第 5 题 · 参考回答Question 5 · Reference answer

记录今天验证/否定的假设、卡住的具体点、下一步和证据位置。不要只写「学了什么」；没有决策与证据，第二周会重复第一周。Record which hypotheses were confirmed or rejected, the exact blocker, the next step and where evidence sits. Do not just log “what I studied”; without decisions and evidence week two repeats week one.

第 6 题 · 参考回答Question 6 · Reference answer

看是否同时要求读理论、搭环境、做评测、写文档和上线；任一项没有可交付物，就会挤压其它项。先删到一条主链路和一个可验收结果。If it demands theory, environment, evaluation, docs and launch at once, and any lacks a deliverable, it will squeeze the rest. Cut to one main pipeline and one acceptance result.

第 7 题 · 参考回答Question 7 · Reference answer

先补失败处理、评测与观测，再补权限和成本。demo 只证明顺利路径能跑；交付要证明失败可发现、可回滚、可解释。Failure handling, evaluation and observability first; then permissions and cost. A demo proves the happy path; delivery proves failure is visible, reversible and explainable.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 8 题 · 参考回答Question 8 · Reference answer

三件事同时变，结果变好或变坏都无法归因。一次只动一个变量，保留旧版本做对照，才能知道是哪一步带来变化。Change all three and you cannot attribute improvement or regression. Move one variable at a time and keep the old version as a control.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 9 题 · 参考回答Question 9 · Reference answer

能对一个未见案例解释决策、能复跑关键结果、能说清失败边界，并留下可检查的产物。只写总结不算证据。You can explain decisions on an unseen case, reproduce the key result, state failure boundaries and leave inspectable artefacts. A written summary alone is not evidence.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 10 题 · 参考回答Question 10 · Reference answer

保留能复用的数据、评测与观测；删掉只服务演示的临时代码和没有验收标准的任务。第二周应围绕一个真实约束加深，而不是横向再开五个主题。Keep reusable data, evaluation and observability; cut demo-only code and tasks without acceptance criteria. Week two should deepen around one real constraint, not open five more topics sideways.
