选型决策者路线The Decision-Maker's Route

落地Ship it部署、上线与团队决策Deploy, operate and decide for a team

约 50 分钟about 50 minutes

把产品、部署、数据、成本与退出条件逐项拆开。目标是形成一份能说明选择原因和未知项的采购记录。Separate product, deployment, data, cost and exit conditions. Aim for a procurement record explaining the choice and unresolved items.

第 1 题 · 参考回答Question 1 · Reference answer

除调用或硬件费，还要算接入、数据处理、维护、监控、复核和迁移。对相同任务量与质量要求计算，避免拿不同范围的报价直接比较。Include integration, data processing, maintenance, monitoring, review and migration beyond API or hardware fees. Compare equal workload and quality requirements.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 2 题 · 参考回答Question 2 · Reference answer

核实数据留存与删除方式、服务可用与退役政策、数据和配置的导出迁移条件；把产品版本、条款链接与核验时间写入记录。Verify retention/deletion, availability/retirement policy, and export/migration of data and configuration. Record product version, terms URL and checking date.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 3 题 · 参考回答Question 3 · Reference answer

相同基准版本和设置下的结果才适合对照；自选演示和未经披露的设置不能直接外推。要求提供失败样本和与你任务相近的评测说明。Compare results with the same benchmark version and settings. Selected demos and undisclosed setups do not support broad conclusions. Ask for failures and task-relevant evaluation details.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 4 题 · 参考回答Question 4 · Reference answer

单价只覆盖调用或硬件本身；还要算接入、数据处理、维护、监控、复核和迁移。按相同任务量与质量要求算总成本，否则报价范围不同，结论会失真。Unit price covers only calls or hardware; integration, data processing, maintenance, monitoring, review and migration also count. Compare total cost at equal workload and quality, or differing scopes distort the conclusion.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 5 题 · 参考回答Question 5 · Reference answer

数据和配置导不出、关键能力只能用厂商私有接口、退役或涨价时没有可执行的替代路径。出现任一条，就应把迁移成本写进采购记录。Data and configuration cannot be exported, key capabilities require proprietary interfaces, or there is no executable alternative when the vendor retires or raises prices. Any one should put migration cost into the procurement record.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 6 题 · 参考回答Question 6 · Reference answer

驻留说明数据存在哪个国家/区域、受哪套法律约束；不出网说明数据是否离开你的边界。前者是位置与合规问题，后者是部署与网络问题，不能互相替代。Residency defines which country or region stores the data and which law applies; “never leaves the network” says whether data crosses your boundary. One is location and compliance, the other deployment and networking; neither substitutes for the other.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 7 题 · 参考回答Question 7 · Reference answer

商用与再分发、衍生作品与权重再发布、用户量与营收等附加条件、专利与商标条款。『开放权重』不等于『无限制使用』。Commercial use and redistribution, derivatives and weight redistribution, thresholds such as users or revenue, and patent/trademark terms. “Open weights” is not “unrestricted use”.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 8 题 · 参考回答Question 8 · Reference answer

榜单通常测公开任务与固定判法，和你的语言、数据、工具链与验收标准不同；版本、污染和提示设置也会改变分数。采购要补自己任务上的小样本评测。Benchmarks test public tasks with a fixed method, unlike your language, data, toolchain and acceptance criteria; version, contamination and prompting also change scores. Add a small evaluation on your own task.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 9 题 · 参考回答Question 9 · Reference answer

常见成功、边界歧义、应拒答/转人工、工具调用与长上下文各一组；再记录接口、流式、成本和回滚。只在普通问答上对比，会漏掉真正会出事故的路径。Typical success, boundary/ambiguous, should-refuse or escalate, tool-calling and long-context sets; also record interface, streaming, cost and rollback. Comparing ordinary Q&amp;A only misses the paths that fail in production.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 10 题 · 参考回答Question 10 · Reference answer

写成「哪一项未确认、缺哪份官方说明、谁负责补、何时复核」，不要写成「应该没问题」。未知项保留在记录里，才能在涨价、退役或事故时追溯。Write which item is unconfirmed, which official statement is missing, who owns it and when to recheck. Do not write “should be fine”. Keeping unknowns traceable matters when prices, retirement or incidents arrive.

走完这一段，去哪练Where to practise after this route

这条路线负责把概念讲通。下一步不用再读一遍；挑一个地方，把刚学的用一次。This route gets the concepts across. Do not reread it; pick one place below and use what you just learned once.

算显存、功耗与总成本Work out VRAM, power and total cost

先把硬件、云租用与电费放在同一张账上，再谈「值不值」。Put hardware, cloud rental and electricity on one bill before arguing whether it is worth it.
