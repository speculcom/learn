运维上线路线The Operator's Route

约 60 分钟about 60 minutes

负责把 AI 服务跑起来并稳住的人：性能从哪来、成本从哪来、出事时先看哪里。For people who keep AI services running: where performance comes from, where cost comes from, and what to check first when things break.

按这个顺序读Read in this order
  1. 推理成本Inference Cost
  2. 首字延迟Time To First Token, TTFT
  3. 吞吐Throughput, TPS
  4. KV 缓存KV Cache
  5. 缓存策略Caching Strategy
  6. 连续批处理Continuous Batching
  7. 速率限制Rate Limiting
  8. 可观测性Observability
  9. 护栏Guardrails
  10. 提示注入Prompt Injection
  11. 权限Permission
  12. 服务中断风险Vendor Dependency
  13. 模型退役Model Retirement
  14. 本地部署Local Deployment
学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 说出首字延迟和吞吐各自受什么影响，各给一个优化手段
  2. 给线上服务列出三件必须监控的事，并说明各自先查什么
  3. 供应商宣布模型退役时，能走一遍迁移评估的四个检查项
  1. Say what drives time-to-first-token and throughput respectively, with one optimization for each
  2. List three things a live service must monitor, and what to check first for each
  3. When a vendor announces retirement, walk through the four migration-assessment checks
