教师与课堂路线The Teacher Route

入门Starter没基础也能读，先建立概念No background needed: build the concepts first

约 40 分钟about 40 minutes

从「AI 能不能进课堂」到「怎么用它备课、出题、批改和互动」。每一步都留一个教师复核点，避免把模型输出直接交给学生。From “can AI enter the classroom” to lesson prep, question generation, grading and interaction. Every step keeps a teacher checkpoint so model output never goes straight to students.

  3. 提示词Prompt

  6. 模型评测的局限Evaluation Limits

  9. 怎么用 AI 出题与批改How to generate questions and grade with AI
  10. 怎么设计课堂 AI 互动How to design AI interaction for the classroom
学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 说清 AI 生成的内容在课堂上哪些可以直接用、哪些必须教师先核
  2. 用一条学习目标写出「导入—讲解—练习—总结」四段活动，并标出 AI 参与点
  3. 给学生一条 AI 可能出错的题目，让他们找出错误并说明理由
  4. 课堂 AI 互动，为什么要先设「不许问」的边界？
  5. 用 AI 出题，如何避免题目只考记忆？
  6. 学生用 AI 写作业，评价应改成看什么？
  7. 备课资料含学生数据，最小化处理是什么？
  8. AI 批改的分数，什么时候必须人工复核？
  9. 把 AI 内容带进课堂，标识与版权怎么讲？
  10. 一节课的 AI 使用，怎么写进学习目标？
  1. Explain which AI-generated classroom content can be used directly and which a teacher must verify first
  2. Turn one learning objective into hook, explanation, practice and summary, marking where AI participates
  3. Give students an AI-generated question that may be wrong and have them find and explain the error
  4. Why set “do not ask” boundaries before AI interaction in class?
  5. How do you avoid AI-generated questions that only test recall?
  6. If students use AI for homework, what should assessment look like?
  7. Lesson material contains student data; what is the minimum handling?
  8. When must an AI-graded score be reviewed by a human?
  9. How do you teach labelling and copyright when bringing AI content into class?
  10. How do you write AI use into a lesson’s learning objectives?

先试着回答，再展开参考答案。答不上来的地方，可从上方步骤返回对应词条。参考回答是核对要点，不要求逐字复述。Try answering before opening the reference. Revisit the relevant step above if needed. These answers highlight key ideas; you do not need to repeat the wording.

第 1 题 · 参考回答Question 1 · Reference answer

事实、公式、日期、例题答案和涉及价值的判断必须教师核；活动创意、分层练习和表达变体可以先由 AI 起草。Facts, formulas, dates, worked answers and values judgments need teacher verification; activity ideas, tiered practice and wording variants can be drafted by AI.

第 2 题 · 参考回答Question 2 · Reference answer

学习目标写「学生能做什么」；四段各标时间、师生活动和检查点；AI 参与点要写清是生成、追问还是反馈。Write the objective as “what students can do”; each phase gets time, teacher/student actions and a checkpoint; state whether AI generates, probes or gives feedback.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 3 题 · 参考回答Question 3 · Reference answer

重点不是「AI 也会错」，而是学生能指出错在条件、计算还是结论，并用教材或原文反驳。The point is not “AI can be wrong” but that students can locate whether the condition, calculation or conclusion failed, and rebut it with the textbook or source.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 4 题 · 参考回答Question 4 · Reference answer

先明确哪些问题不该交给外部工具（学生隐私、考试题、未公开资料），再谈怎么用。没有边界，互动会先泄露数据，再谈学习效果。Define what must not go to an external tool (student privacy, exam items, unpublished material) before discussing use. Without boundaries, interaction leaks data before learning effects matter.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 5 题 · 参考回答Question 5 · Reference answer

要求题目给新材料、让学生解释理由、比较两种方案或找出错误；答案要能按 rubric 判，而不是只对知识点。生成后自己先做一遍，删掉歧义题。Ask for new material, explanations, comparisons or error-finding; answers must be rubric-scorable, not just recall. Solve them yourself and delete ambiguous items.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 6 题 · 参考回答Question 6 · Reference answer

看过程证据：提纲、修改记录、来源核对与口头解释；再看最终作品。只评成品无法区分谁写的，也无法教方法。Look at process evidence: outlines, revision history, source checks and oral explanation, then the final work. Product-only grading cannot distinguish authorship or teach method.

第 7 题 · 参考回答Question 7 · Reference answer

去掉姓名与可识别组合，只保留教学需要的字段；外部工具只用脱敏样本，并确认留存与删除。不要把原始班级表直接上传。Remove names and identifying combinations, keep only fields needed for teaching, use masked samples in external tools and confirm retention/deletion. Do not upload the raw class roster.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 8 题 · 参考回答Question 8 · Reference answer

涉及升学/奖惩、主观题、边界分数、疑似抄袭或学生申诉时。机器分只能当初筛，最终分数要有可解释的 rubric 与复核人。When it affects progression, rewards, subjective items, borderline scores, suspected plagiarism or appeals. Machine scores are a first pass; final scores need an explainable rubric and reviewer.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 9 题 · 参考回答Question 9 · Reference answer

讲清哪些内容必须标 AI 生成、哪些素材不能直接商用、引用要回到原页。让学生用一张「来源/许可/标识」三列表检查自己的作品。Teach which content must be labelled AI-generated, which assets cannot be used commercially, and that citations must return to the original. Have students check their work with a source/licence/label table.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 10 题 · 参考回答Question 10 · Reference answer

写成可观察的行为：学生能用 AI 生成初稿、核对两条来源、指出一处错误并说明理由。不要写「会用 AI」这种无法验收的目标。Write observable behaviour: students draft with AI, verify two sources, identify one error and explain why. Avoid unassessable goals such as “can use AI”.
