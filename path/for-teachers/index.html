教师与课堂路线The Teacher Route

约 40 分钟about 40 minutes

从「AI 能不能进课堂」到「怎么用它备课、出题、批改和互动」。每一步都留一个教师复核点，避免把模型输出直接交给学生。From “can AI enter the classroom” to lesson prep, question generation, grading and interaction. Every step keeps a teacher checkpoint so model output never goes straight to students.

学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 说清 AI 生成的内容在课堂上哪些可以直接用、哪些必须教师先核
  2. 用一条学习目标写出「导入—讲解—练习—总结」四段活动，并标出 AI 参与点
  3. 给学生一条 AI 可能出错的题目，让他们找出错误并说明理由
  1. Explain which AI-generated classroom content can be used directly and which a teacher must verify first
  2. Turn one learning objective into hook, explanation, practice and summary, marking where AI participates
  3. Give students an AI-generated question that may be wrong and have them find and explain the error

第 1 题 · 参考回答Question 1 · Reference answer

事实、公式、日期、例题答案和涉及价值的判断必须教师核；活动创意、分层练习和表达变体可以先由 AI 起草。Facts, formulas, dates, worked answers and values judgments need teacher verification; activity ideas, tiered practice and wording variants can be drafted by AI.

第 2 题 · 参考回答Question 2 · Reference answer

学习目标写「学生能做什么」；四段各标时间、师生活动和检查点；AI 参与点要写清是生成、追问还是反馈。Write the objective as “what students can do”; each phase gets time, teacher/student actions and a checkpoint; state whether AI generates, probes or gives feedback.

第 3 题 · 参考回答Question 3 · Reference answer

重点不是「AI 也会错」，而是学生能指出错在条件、计算还是结论，并用教材或原文反驳。The point is not “AI can be wrong” but that students can locate whether the condition, calculation or conclusion failed, and rebut it with the textbook or source.
