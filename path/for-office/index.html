办公与写作路线The Office Route

入门Starter没基础也能读，先建立概念No background needed: build the concepts first

约 45 分钟about 45 minutes

从「把 AI 当搜索框」走到「把 AI 接进文档、表格、会议与邮件」。先认清能力边界，再按场景练一遍完整流程。Move from using AI as a search box to wiring it into documents, spreadsheets, meetings and email. Learn the limits first, then rehearse one complete workflow per scenario.

学完自测Checkpoint
  1. 拿到一份会议转写，能标出哪些句子必须回原文核对、哪些可以直接进纪要
  2. 说清一次 PPT/表格任务里，AI 负责哪几步、人负责哪几步
  3. 判断一条办公数据能否贴进云端模型，依据是数据驻留与授权而不是「大家都这么用」
  4. 会议纪要里，哪些内容必须标「待确认」？
  5. 用 AI 做 PPT，先给大纲还是先给模板？
  6. 表格分析中，AI 最常把什么算错？
  7. 邮件让 AI 代写，发送前必须检查哪三项？
  8. 合同审阅为什么不能只要「风险清单」？
  9. 翻译本地化，什么该译、什么不该译？
  10. 把客户数据交给 AI 清洗，最小脱敏规则是什么？
  1. Given a meeting transcript, mark which sentences must be checked against the transcript and which can go straight into minutes
  2. Explain which steps AI owns and which a human owns in a deck or spreadsheet task
  3. Decide whether office data can go into a cloud model based on residency and licensing, not on “everyone does it”
  4. What must be marked “to confirm” in meeting minutes?
  5. For AI-assisted slides, start with the outline or the template?
  6. What does AI most often get wrong in table analysis?
  7. Before sending an AI-drafted email, what three checks are mandatory?
  8. Why is a “risk list” not enough for contract review?
  9. In localisation, what should and should not be translated?
  10. What is the minimum de-identification rule before giving customer data to AI for cleaning?

先试着回答，再展开参考答案。答不上来的地方，可从上方步骤返回对应词条。参考回答是核对要点，不要求逐字复述。Try answering before opening the reference. Revisit the relevant step above if needed. These answers highlight key ideas; you do not need to repeat the wording.

第 1 题 · 参考回答Question 1 · Reference answer

数字、承诺、姓名、日期和「谁负责」必须回原文；总结性描述可以基于转写生成，但也要能指回原句。Numbers, commitments, names, dates and ownership must trace to the transcript; summaries can be generated but must still point back to a line.

第 2 题 · 参考回答Question 2 · Reference answer

AI 负责结构、初稿、变体和检查清单；人负责目标、取舍、事实核对和对外发送。AI owns structure, first drafts, variants and checklists; the human owns goals, trade-offs, fact-checking and sending.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 3 题 · 参考回答Question 3 · Reference answer

先看数据分类、合同/合规要求、模型是否留存与跨境；不能确认时改用本地模型、脱敏或不上传。Check data class, contract/compliance rules, retention and cross-border transfer. If unclear, use a local model, mask the data or do not upload.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 4 题 · 参考回答Question 4 · Reference answer

没有明确负责人、截止日期、数字口径或决策依据的条目。纪要不是逐字稿；把猜测写成结论，会比漏记更危险。Items without an owner, deadline, numeric definition or decision basis. Minutes are not transcripts; writing a guess as a conclusion is more dangerous than omitting it.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 5 题 · 参考回答Question 5 · Reference answer

先定大纲与每页结论，再套模板。模板先行会让内容迁就版式；大纲先行才能检查叙事是否完整、每页是否有证据。Fix the outline and the point of each slide first, then apply the template. Template-first makes content serve layout; outline-first exposes gaps and evidence.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 6 题 · 参考回答Question 6 · Reference answer

口径（去重、缺失、时间窗）、行列方向、单位和总计。让它先复述口径与样本数，再给结果；关键数字要回到原始表复核。Definitions (deduplication, missing values, time window), row/column orientation, units and totals. Ask it to restate definitions and sample counts before results; recheck key numbers against the sheet.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 7 题 · 参考回答Question 7 · Reference answer

收件人与抄送、承诺/数字/日期、语气与保密边界。尤其检查它有没有替你做出未经授权的承诺。Recipients and cc, commitments/numbers/dates, tone and confidentiality. Especially check whether it made an commitment you were not authorised to make.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 8 题 · 参考回答Question 8 · Reference answer

清单不给条款位置、适用条件和修改建议，业务无法据此谈判。要求它逐条引用原文、标出双方义务与退出条件，再由法务复核。A list without clause location, conditions and proposed edits cannot support negotiation. Require verbatim clauses, obligations and exit terms, then have legal review.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 9 题 · 参考回答Question 9 · Reference answer

界面、说明、法律与营销文案要译；品牌名、代码、API 字段、法规编号和商标通常保留，除非官方有本地译名。术语表先行，避免同一词多种译法。UI, instructions, legal and marketing copy translate; brand names, code, API fields, regulation numbers and trademarks usually stay, unless an official local name exists. Build a glossary first to avoid inconsistent terms.

回看这一题的概念Revisit this concept

第 10 题 · 参考回答Question 10 · Reference answer

去掉姓名、电话、身份证、地址、账号等直接标识，能聚合就聚合；保留分析需要的字段，并记录脱敏人与还原权限。不要用「假名化」代替删除。Remove direct identifiers such as names, phones, ID numbers, addresses and accounts; aggregate where possible; keep only fields needed for analysis and record who masked and who can re-identify. Pseudonymising is not deletion.
