Automation Bias

Automation Bias

  • Core concepts
  • Stage 2 · Hands On
  • refs checked 2026-11-03
What problem it solves

The human-side flaw: smooth AI output makes people skip checking and accept it wholesale.

What it is
The International AI Safety Report notes people exhibit automation bias — accepting AI suggestions without checking them carefully. It is distinct from model bias: one is the model being wrong, the other is how humans use it. The more fluent and confident the output and the more authoritative the interface, the more people relax their checking. Process countermeasure: mandatory second verification for high-stakes decisions, not reliance on seeming reliable.
Why it matters

Model error is only half of many incidents; the other half is humans no longer verifying. Knowing this flaw is why critical workflows need enforced checkpoints.
