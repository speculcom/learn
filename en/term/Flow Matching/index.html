Flow Matching

Flow Matching

  • Image, speech & video
  • Stage 6 · Choosing Right
  • refs checked 2026-11-18
What problem it solves

Learn a continuous velocity field from noise to data and push noise into samples with an ODE.

What it is
Training regresses the velocity from a noise point toward a data point, rather than predicting noise or pixels directly. Inference starts from noise and integrates the velocity field for a number of steps. A continuous generative framework beside diffusion; Stable Diffusion 3 and Flux use rectified-flow variants.
Why it matters

Diffusion needs many sampling steps; Flow Matching learns a straighter path, so fewer steps can suffice. It explains why image and video generators moved from “diffusion” to “flow” in 2025–2026.
