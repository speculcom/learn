Bias

Bias

  • Core concepts
  • Stage 2 · Hands On
  • refs checked 2026-11-03
What problem it solves

Uneven model performance across groups, languages, and cultures — rooted in training data.

What it is
The International AI Safety Report notes performance declines in languages and cultural contexts less common in training data. The unevenness is attributed directly to the distribution of training data. Stereotypes carried by the corpus are learned and reproduced by the model. Humans accepting AI suggestions without checking (automation bias) is a separate layer — don't conflate it with model bias.
Why it matters

The same model is not equally good for everyone. When evaluating an AI product, 'how does it perform in your context' is a required question.
