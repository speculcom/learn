Diff

Diff

  • AI coding
  • Stage 3 · Going Deeper
  • refs checked 2026-11-09
What problem it solves

See exactly what the AI changed — the most important step when accepting AI-written code.

What it is
Comparing before and after line by line, the basic means of reviewing AI output.
Why it matters

A pattern that recurs in official guidance: keep AI changes under version control, diffable and revertible. That is a precondition for safety.

Common misuse

A diff shows what changed, not whether it is right. Run tests and type checks before accepting the change.
