Pre-launch Gates for AI Features

Pre-launch Gates for AI Features

  • Selection & cost
  • Stage 6 · Choosing Right
  • refs checked 2026-11-06
What problem it solves

Turns safety requirements into gates that block release until they pass.

What it is
A gate has to be decidable: a threshold, an artefact, an owner, and a stated consequence when it fails. "Output quality is good" is not a gate; "the feature's evaluation set was run, and every failed case is logged and assigned" is. Thresholds come from measurement. The NIST AI Risk Management Framework puts MEASURE next to MANAGE, which is the same point: an auditable measurement basis and a recorded risk response come first, release criteria second. "Safe" without a measurement method cannot support a go decision. Check coverage against a known catalogue. The OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (official source repository, 2026 edition, LLM01 to LLM10) works as a per-item confirmation sheet: is the mitigation deployed, was it tested, when. Six gate categories come up repeatedly in practice: evaluation results with failing samples, a re-check of privileges granted to tools, logging and retention settings including whether secrets reach logs, a rollback path rehearsed once, a named owner for degradation and kill decisions, and written waivers for risks left uncovered. Write automatic fallback conditions into the gate: which signals - refusal-rate or cost anomalies, policy-violating output, complaint volume crossing a line - trigger a downgrade without calling a meeting. Invented teaching example, not a real threshold: a team puts "pass rate at or above the agreed target on this feature's evaluation set, and zero failures on high-severity cases" into its release flow, and records where the number came from and when it was last reviewed on the release ticket. Set your own thresholds against your own evaluation set; the example figures are not transferable.
Why it matters

A gate is useful only if failing it stops the release. The column that is usually missing is "what happens if it fails" - and without it, business pressure walks straight through the gate on the last day, and afterwards nobody can say who let it through or on what basis.
