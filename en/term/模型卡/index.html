Model Card

Model Card

  • Selection & cost
  • Stage 1 · Getting In
  • refs checked 2026-11-09
What problem it solves

A model's 'spec sheet': intended use, measured performance, known shortcomings — issued with the model.

What it is
The model card concept was proposed by Mitchell et al. in 2019: short documents accompanying released models. They report intended use, benchmarked performance across groups and conditions, evaluation procedures, and known limitations. The motivation: reduce misuse of models in contexts they are not suited for. Major model releases now commonly ship model cards or equivalent documentation.
Why it matters

When choosing a model, don't stop at the name and scores. The model card is the vendor's own admitted shortcoming list — more honest than any review.
