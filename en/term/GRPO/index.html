Group Relative Policy Optimization

Group Relative Policy Optimization

  • Core concepts
  • Stage 5 · Running It
  • refs checked 2026-11-18
What problem it solves

Compare a group of answers to the same question instead of training a separate value network to estimate the baseline.

What it is
Sample a group of answers per question, use the group mean as the baseline, and compute each answer’s relative advantage. It is reinforcement learning without the PPO critic, a value network as large as the policy. Proposed in DeepSeekMath; DeepSeek-R1 brought it into large-scale reasoning post-training.
Why it matters

The expensive part of post-training is judging how much better one answer is than average. GRPO turns that into a within-group relative value, cutting memory and engineering complexity; it is how “reasoning is trained” actually works.
