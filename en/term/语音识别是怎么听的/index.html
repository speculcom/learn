How Speech Recognition Works

How Speech Recognition Works

  • Image, speech & video
  • Stage 4 · Under the Hood
  • refs checked 2026-11-09
What problem it solves

The mainstream way to turn speech into text: large-scale weak supervision, one model for many accents.

What it is
Whisper (OpenAI, 2022) trains on 680,000 hours of web audio-video with weak supervision; one model covers many languages and accents. It turns recognition into a translation-style task: audio goes into an encoder, text comes out as decoded output word by word. The cost of weak supervision: training data has no human proofreading, so transcripts fail on specialist terms and accent edges. The evaluation metric is word error rate (WER): wrong words divided by total words — lower is better.
Why it matters

The error rate of the speech stage directly determines the quality of everything after it. Knowing it is statistical transcription rather than understanding tells you where to add a safety net.
