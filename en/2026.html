Four terms for four lines that are usually skipped

The glossary moves from 256 to 260 — not padding: training (GRPO), inference (MLA), representation (JEPA) and generation (Flow Matching) each had a frequently mentioned concept without a standalone entry.

Each entry gives sources, boundaries and what it does not claim; after reading, you can tell which layer a product page is talking about instead of memorising another acronym.

Four new terms

Ordered training → inference → representation → generation, not alphabetically. Each links to its own term page.

TRAININGGRPO
INFERENCEMLA
REPRESENTATIONJEPA
GENERATIONFlow Matching
01Training

GRPO →

Compare a group of answers to one question instead of training a PPO critic. Understand what “reasoning is trained” actually trains.

Boundary

Not reward-model-free; group size and reward design drive stability.

02Inference

MLA →

Compresses KV cache into a low-rank latent, relaxing the first wall in long context.

Boundary

Saves memory and bandwidth, not FLOPs; not infinite context.

03Representation

JEPA →

Predicts masked parts in representation space rather than reconstructing pixels or tokens; JEPA-PRH uses it to test cross-modal alignment in 2026.

Boundary

Alignment is not generation quality; the preprint is limited in scale.

04Generation

Flow Matching →

Learns a velocity field from noise to data and samples by ODE integration; one mainstream basis for few-step generation.

Boundary

Not one-step generation; steps depend on distillation, and FID is not human preference.
