The 2026 Second-Half Timeline

Guide · about 10 min · refs checked 2026-11-18 (reference links and dates only)

Official update snapshots: dates, rollout scope and planned shutdowns, with sources; ordered newest event first.

How to
Tools: Opal is scheduled to shut down on 2026-11-17 without automatic workflow migration; preserve prompts and files. Vercel AI SDK 7 appears in its 2026 changelog. Protocols and hardware: AMD MI450X launched on 2026-11-12; Agent Skills was donated to AAIF on 2026-10-22; ExLlamaV3 1.6.5 shipped on 2026-10-25. Models: Mistral Large 4 (2026-10-29, 1.05T / 49B active), Meta Muse Spark Open (2026-10-15, 816B / 42B active) and Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6 (2026-09-22) opened weights in sequence. Research 2: the JEPA-PRH preprint reported cross-modal alignment for a 109M text JEPA on 2026-10-29; Byteification reached Nature on 2026-10-07, converting a subword model to bytes with 49.1B tokens. Model tiers: Claude Duet opened a Pro/Max research preview on 2026-10-21; Gemini 4 Argon began rolling out to AI Pro/Ultra on 2026-10-13. Image and retirements: Microsoft retired consumer Copilot Deep Research on 2026-08-18; the ChatGPT Atlas browser was retired on 2026-08-09 with capabilities moved into ChatGPT/Codex; Nano Banana 2 launched on 2026-02-26. Research: Google shipped Deep Research and Deep Research Max on 2026-04-21, built on Gemini 3.1 Pro with MCP and custom sources.
