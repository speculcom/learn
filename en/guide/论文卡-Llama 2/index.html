Paper Card - Llama 2

Paper card · about 7 min · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

The landmark tech report for open-weight chat models. This card lays out what it set out to solve, where the evidence sits, and what it does not claim.

What to take away
Research question: the abstract announces "a collection of pretrained and fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) ranging in scale from 7 billion to 70 billion parameters"; the fine-tuned Llama 2-Chat is "optimized for dialogue use cases". Core evidence: Section 3's fine-tuning pipeline (SFT + rejection sampling + PPO) and Section 4's safety work — the abstract itself frames the contribution as "We provide a detailed description of our approach to fine-tuning and safety improvements"; the comparison claim carries a qualifier: "outperform open-source chat models on most benchmarks we tested" (most, not all). Author-stated limits: no standalone Limitations section (checked against the arXiv HTML version on 2026-10-29); the closest are Section 5 Discussion (trade-offs and risks) and Section 4 Safety (with a content warning) — discussion, not a limitations list. Scope of validity is the reader's job. What it does not claim: the abstract says "may be a suitable substitute for closed-source models" — never a claim of surpassing them. And open-weight is not open-source: the community license carries use restrictions (see the Open weights term). How to read it: the core contribution is the openness of the fine-tuning and safety process, not the benchmark scores; reading only the eval tables misses what the paper actually gives.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

Record the paper's training method separately from the actual downloaded model's license. Check version, terms and evaluation setup even within one family; the family name is not verification.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
