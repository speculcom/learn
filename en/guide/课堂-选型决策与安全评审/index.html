Lesson - Selection Decisions and Security Review

Lesson plan · about 15 min · refs checked 2026-11-07 (reference links and dates only)

Learners consolidate evidence from the first five units into a one-page decision record, review it against an official risk framework and prompt-injection guidance, and write unverified items and a review date.

Lesson objectives
Write a one-page decision record: task, candidates, evidence, risks and mitigations, unverified items, review date. Check candidate use, limits, training data and evaluation conditions in model-card terms, writing unknown for missing items. Classify the record under GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE, and identify which column is still empty. Give prompt-injection mitigations including least privilege and human approval for high-risk actions.
Materials
NIST AI RMF official page and AI RMF 1.0 text: the four functions in Part 2. OWASP GenAI LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection entry: causes and mitigations. arXiv 1810.03993 Model Cards for Model Reporting abstract: what model cards should contain. This site’s guides on reading model cards and preventing prompt injection as pre-class reading. A one-page decision-record template with the six columns from the first objective.
Steps
    3-minute task definition: write decidable conditions including inputs, outputs, acceptable error range and reviewer role. 4-minute evidence: for each candidate write only content that links to a source; write unknown for missing items. 4-minute review: list risks, mitigations and responsible roles under the four functions and the prompt-injection entry. 4-minute exit: give exit conditions and a review date; groups peer-review which column is most often skipped.
Common misconceptions
Assuming a high evaluation score means ready to launch; evaluation conditions may differ from your task conditions. Assuming guardrails remove risk; permissions, logs and human review still sit outside the guardrail. Assuming a compliance framework decides for you; NIST AI RMF is a voluntary framework that gives structure, not conclusions. Assuming open source or local deployment is naturally safer; licence, data path and access control still need item-by-item checks.
Assessment checks
The decision record has all six columns, unverified items are explicit, and the review date is actionable. Every risk has a mitigation and verification method, with at least one involving permissions or human approval. Learners can state the record’s boundary: classroom review does not replace legal advice and is not launch approval.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

Class exercise: a candidate tool has a high evaluation score but its model card lacks training data and evaluation conditions. Write unknown in the decision record and list least privilege plus human approval for high-risk actions as mitigations.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
