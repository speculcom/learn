Attention Means It Notices What Matters

Guide · about 5 min · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

Attention is a weighted computation, not the model paying heed.

"Attention mechanism" means it picks out what matters, like a person would.

Attention weights are computational quantities, not direct equivalents of human attention or causal explanations. Visualizations aid observation; conclusions still need controlled checks or other validation.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

A higher attention weight does not establish author-intended importance or a causal explanation. To investigate reliance on a passage, design controlled input changes and inspect the output.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
