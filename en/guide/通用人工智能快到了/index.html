AGI Is Just Around the Corner

Guide · about 5 min · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

"Soon" is a claim, not a verifiable milestone.

Everyone says AGI is near, so there must be a checkable date when it arrives.

AGI definitions and evaluation criteria are not settled. A claim of proximity needs specified capabilities, test conditions and completion criteria; a research goal or forecast does not establish achievement.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

For “AGI next year,” ask which capabilities AGI means, how they are measured, who checks them, and whether the date is a commitment or prediction. Without those answers, record a prediction rather than a planning assumption.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
