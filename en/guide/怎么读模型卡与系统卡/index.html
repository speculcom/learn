How to Read Model Cards and System Cards

Guide · about 8 min · refs checked 2026-11-06 (reference links and dates only)

Model cards say what a model is and how to use it; system cards and deployment safety pages say what was tested before release and what still worries the publisher. Read them separately, and side by side.

How to
Tell the two apart by audience: model cards face users (intended use, limitations, evaluation scope, training-data notes); system cards and deployment safety pages face risk readers (which risks were measured, with what result, under what conditions the rollout continues). Check what was measured and what was not: a document with only capability benchmarks and no failure-mode discussion cannot support a high-risk use; one that only discusses risk without checkable numbers is no better. Record version and date together: results for the same model name can differ across card releases, so put the version and publication date in your notes when you cite it. Read the "not suitable for" section closely: failure cases and refusal behaviour the publisher writes down are more usable than marketing lines; a card without that section deserves discount. Map gaps onto your own use: if your scenario is outside its evaluation coverage, that gap is yours to test - extrapolation does not fill it. Worked example (fictional need): input is a team wanting a model to draft medical Q&A answers -> processing reads the model card and system card side by side and lists "is there any medical evaluation" and "which section states known refusals and hallucination behaviour" -> the judgement is that if neither document covers the scenario, the conclusion reads "cards insufficient to support this use" -> next step: add the scenario to your own evaluation samples, run a small trial with records, then decide whether to integrate.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

When a card says 'harmful output remains possible after safety training', note which risk categories it covers, how they were evaluated, and what is excluded. List the missing parts as unknowns instead of inferring protection strength from the version number.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
