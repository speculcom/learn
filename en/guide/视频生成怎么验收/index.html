How to Acceptance-Test AI Video Generation

Guide · about 9 min · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

Video acceptance is not “it looks right”. Score picture, motion, temporal order and audio-visual sync separately.

How to
Set delivery criteria first: duration, resolution, frame rate, aspect ratio, watermark; reject anything that misses. Picture: subject consistency, correct text and logos, high-risk areas such as hands and faces. Motion: does the camera move as prompted, is the physics plausible, are there jumps or flicker? Temporal: do character, costume and scene stay consistent across cuts? Check the seams in long videos. Audio-visual: with dialogue or effects, check lip sync, sound-picture sync and room tone. Editability: can it be partially regenerated, extended or re-referenced? A piece you cannot edit is expensive to redo. Log every failed sample and its parameters into a regression set; do not keep only the best take.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

Produce an 8-second product clip: fix 1080p, 24fps, 9:16 and no watermark first. Then check logo distortion, whether the push-in follows the prompt, label consistency across shots, and lip-sync against the voiceover. Log the settings for any failure and rerun.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
