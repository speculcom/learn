Local Deployment Means Compliance

Guide · about 5 min · refs checked 2026-11-06 (reference links and dates only)

Keeping data on your own machines addresses where data flows, not what obligations apply.

Deploy the model locally and compliance is automatically solved.

Compliance follows the business, not the location of the compute: intended use, who the service faces, the authorisation chain for the data, content labeling and record keeping. Local deployment adds its own items - weight and license terms, which internal data gets connected, and who is allowed to use which model. What to verify is how the service is classified and which duties still apply, not whether the machine is yours. (Fictional example) Input is the decision to move the same externally facing summarisation service from cloud to a local model -> processing still checks three things: who the service faces, whether published generated content needs labeling, and how far the internal data authorisation reaches -> if any one of them still applies, the conclusion "local deployment, therefore no duties" is wrong -> the next step is to have legal confirm the classification and to write the labeling practice into the release check. Final obligations follow the text of the rule.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

A model running on your own machine can still leak through cloud logging, plugin callbacks or operator shell access. Compliance follows data flows and authorization records, not the physical location of the box.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
