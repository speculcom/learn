How to review contracts and long documents with AI

Guide · about 8 min · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

Let AI find clauses, flag risks and compare versions; do not let it issue a legal conclusion.

How to
Set a review checklist first: payment, breach, termination, liability cap, IP, data and confidentiality, governing law. Split by clause number; ask for “location + risk + suggested redline” per clause, not a summary. Use a two-version diff to surface additions, deletions and wording changes; list formatting changes separately. Every risk flag must point to a page or clause; anything without a source becomes “human review required”. Route output to counsel; do not send externally. AI is not legal advice. Pull out personal-data, cross-border data and AI-training-rights clauses for a separate compliance pass.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

Take a 12-page service contract, have the model flag risks under payment, breach, termination and liability cap; every flag needs a clause number, unsourced ones become “human review”, then route to counsel.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
