How to Check Generative AI Compliance Duties

Guide · about 8 min · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

Launching an AI feature in China, compliance is not mysticism but a handful of checkable articles. This guide walks them in launch order.

How to
Classify first: does the service face the public in mainland China? If so, the Interim Measures for Generative AI Services apply. Check training-data provenance: Article 7 requires "lawfully sourced data and foundation models". Check labeling duties: generated images and video must carry labels per the deep-synthesis rules. Check filing: services with public-opinion attributes or social mobilization capacity need security assessments and algorithm filing. Check the new labeling rules: the Labeling Measures take effect 1 September 2025 — explicit labels plus implicit labels in file metadata. When unsure, ask legal: this site states what the articles say, it does not hand down legal conclusions for you.

Example and interpretation

A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

Describe users, service region, processed data and output publication before checking official scope provisions. The worksheet organizes questions; labels such as “internal” or “API wrapper” alone do not determine obligations.

How to check your practice

Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
