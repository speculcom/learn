闭源模型

Closed / Proprietary Model

  • 选型与成本Selection & cost
  • 第六层 · 选型层Layer 6 · Choosing
解决什么问题What problem it solves

你不拿到权重，只能通过官方接口调用。You do not get the weights; you can only call it through the official interface.

这是什么What it is

模型不公开权重，只能走官方提供的 API 或产品使用。

Models whose weights are not published, usable only through the provider's API or product.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

通常更强、更新更快，但数据要外发、成本按量付费、有服务中断风险。Usually stronger and updated faster, but data leaves your machine, you pay per use, and you carry outage risk.
