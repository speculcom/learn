DPO（直接偏好优化）DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)

DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)

  • 模型能力Model capabilities
  • 第三段 · 用深Stage 3 · Going Deeper
  • 引用核验 2026-11-17refs checked 2026-11-17
解决什么问题What problem it solves

用成对的「更好 / 更差」回答直接微调模型，省掉奖励模型和强化学习。Fine-tune directly on paired better and worse answers, without a reward model or reinforcement learning.

这是什么What it is

DPO 把 RLHF 的目标改写成分类损失。提高优选回答的概率、降低劣选回答的概率。

它不需要在训练中从模型采样，也不需要单独训练奖励模型。

DPO rewrites the RLHF objective as a classification loss: raise the probability of the preferred answer and lower the rejected one.

It needs neither sampling from the model during training nor a separately trained reward model.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

想让模型更符合偏好但不想搭 PPO 那一套时，DPO 是更轻的选择。偏好数据的覆盖面仍然决定效果上限。When you want preference alignment without the PPO machinery, DPO is the lighter option. The coverage of preference data still caps the result.
