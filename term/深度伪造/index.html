深度伪造Deepfake

Deepfake

  • 图像语音视频Image, speech & video
  • 第二段 · 上手Stage 2 · Hands On
  • 引用核验 2026-11-03refs checked 2026-11-03
解决什么问题What problem it solves

用生成模型造出以假乱真的真人音视频：眼见不再为实。Generative models producing indistinguishable fake video and audio of real people: seeing is no longer believing.

这是什么What it is

深度伪造指用深度学习合成或篡改的图像、音频、视频。人难以辨真伪，且常以真实人物为对象。

综述指出：deepfake 算法能造出与真实影像无法区分的假内容。

检测与生成是一场持续军备竞赛，检测器普遍滞后于生成器。

滥用场景包括冒充真人诈骗、伪造事件影像。

Deepfakes are deep-learning-synthesized or manipulated images, audio, or video that humans struggle to distinguish from authentic ones, often targeting real people.

Surveys: deepfake algorithms can create fake media indistinguishable from authentic media.

Detection and generation are an ongoing arms race; detectors generally lag generators.

Abuse scenarios include impersonation scams and fabricated footage of events.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

看到「实拍」视频先问一句有没有可能是合成的。涉及钱和决策的音视频，要换渠道二次确认。When you see 'real footage', ask first whether it could be synthetic. For audio and video involving money or decisions, verify through a second channel.
