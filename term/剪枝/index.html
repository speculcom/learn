剪枝

Pruning

  • 硬件与部署Hardware & deployment
  • 第五站 · 跑起来Station 5 · Running It
解决什么问题What problem it solves

删掉模型里不重要的连接，让它变小变快。Delete the unimportant connections in a model to make it smaller and faster.

这是什么What it is

把权重接近零的连接去掉，模型更小、推理更快。

论文实测：对预训练模型做结构化剪枝，精度损失可以很小。

Removing weights close to zero makes the model smaller and inference faster.

Measured in the paper: structured pruning of a pretrained model can lose very little accuracy.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

它和量化是两条互补的压缩路线。本地跑不动的模型，有时靠剪枝救回来。It is a compression route complementary to quantisation. A model that will not fit locally is sometimes rescued by pruning.
