Data Residency

  • 选型与成本Selection & cost
  • 第六段 · 选得对Stage 6 · Choosing Right
  • 引用核验 2026-11-09refs checked 2026-11-09
解决什么问题What problem it solves

你的数据存在哪个区域，由谁决定。Which region your data sits in, and who decides.

这是什么What it is

AWS 官方：客户选择在哪个 AWS 区域存储客户内容。

官方称除提供服务所必需或依法要求外，不会把内容移出所选区域。

AWS states that the customer chooses the AWS Region(s) where customer content is stored.

AWS says it will not move or replicate content outside the chosen region(s) except as necessary to provide the service or comply with law.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

「数据不出网」在云端的具体形态。合规审查时这是要拿书面承诺的一项。The cloud form of "data never leaves our network", and a written commitment to collect during review.
