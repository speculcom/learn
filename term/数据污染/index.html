数据污染Data Contamination

Data Contamination

  • 核心概念Core concepts
  • 第四段 · 看懂Stage 4 · Under the Hood
  • 引用核验 2026-11-09refs checked 2026-11-09
解决什么问题What problem it solves

评测题混进训练数据，模型「考过原题」——评测分数因此失真。Eval questions leaking into training data — models have 'seen the exam', so scores become distorted.

这是什么What it is

指评测集的内容在预训练阶段混进了训练数据，被模型提前见过。

GPT-3 论文自己就做过 n-gram 重叠检查，评估各评测集被污染的影响。

Magar 与 Schwartz 进一步把污染分成两种行为：「记住」与「利用」。该研究对两者做了量化。

静态评测集公开可得，污染几乎不可避免。

Contamination means evaluation-set content was inadvertently seen during pre-training.

The GPT-3 paper itself ran n-gram overlap checks to assess contamination across eval sets.

Magar & Schwartz split contamination into memorization and exploitation, and quantified both.

With static eval sets publicly available, contamination is nearly unavoidable.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

榜单上「某模型在 X 基准特别高」，可能只是它见过原题。评测评判模型前先问：这个基准会不会已经漏进训练数据。'Model X tops benchmark Y' may just mean it saw the questions. Before trusting a leaderboard slot, ask whether that benchmark has already leaked into training data.
