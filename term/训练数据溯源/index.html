训练数据溯源Training data provenance

Training data provenance

  • 数据与检索Data & retrieval
  • 第五段 · 跑起来Stage 5 · Running It
  • 引用核验 2026-11-19refs checked 2026-11-19
解决什么问题What problem it solves

记录一条训练样本从哪来、经过哪些清洗/变换、进了哪个训练集。Record where a training sample came from, what cleaning or transformation it went through and which dataset it entered.

这是什么What it is

溯源至少要有原始 URL、抓取时间、许可证、处理脚本与版本。

多源混合后仍要能回答「这条样本来自哪里」。

它是数据授权、删除请求与污染排查的基础。

Provenance needs at least the original URL, crawl time, licence, processing script and version.

After blending sources, the system must still answer “where did this sample come from”.

It underpins licensing, deletion requests and contamination investigation.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

模型答错或泄露时，第一步就是回溯它可能见过什么数据。没有溯源就只能猜。When a model leaks or misbehaves, the first step is retracing what it may have seen. Without provenance you can only guess.
