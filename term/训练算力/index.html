  • 第四层 · 原理层Layer 4 · Why it behaves this way
解决什么问题What problem it solves

训练和推理都要烧算力，这是 AI 最硬的资源门槛。Both training and inference burn compute. This is AI's hardest resource threshold.

这是什么What it is

训练需要大量 GPU 和时间；推理便宜得多但持续消耗。

Training needs many GPUs and a lot of time; inference is far cheaper but costs continuously.
为什么需要知道Why it matters

它解释了为什么大模型公司烧钱、为什么本地跑大模型要先算显存账。It explains why large-model companies burn money, and why running one locally means doing the VRAM arithmetic first.
