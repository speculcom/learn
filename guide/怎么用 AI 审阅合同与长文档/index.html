怎么用 AI 审阅合同与长文档How to review contracts and long documents with AI

微型指南 · 约 8 分钟Guide · about 8 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

让 AI 做「找条款、标风险、对比版本」，不让他替你下法律结论。Let AI find clauses, flag risks and compare versions; do not let it issue a legal conclusion.

怎么做How to

先定审阅清单：付款、违约、终止、责任上限、知识产权、数据与保密、管辖法律。

把合同按条款编号切块，让模型逐条输出「原文位置 + 风险 + 建议改写」，不要只给摘要。

用两版对比模式找新增/删除/措辞变化；格式变化单独列出，避免淹没实质修改。

每个风险标记必须回到原文页码/条款号；找不到原文的判断一律标「待人工确认」。

输出给律师/法务复核，不直接对外发送；AI 不是法律意见。

涉及个人信息、跨境数据、AI 训练权的条款单独拉出来，按合规要求再看一遍。

Set a review checklist first: payment, breach, termination, liability cap, IP, data and confidentiality, governing law.

Split by clause number; ask for “location + risk + suggested redline” per clause, not a summary.

Use a two-version diff to surface additions, deletions and wording changes; list formatting changes separately.

Every risk flag must point to a page or clause; anything without a source becomes “human review required”.

Route output to counsel; do not send externally. AI is not legal advice.

Pull out personal-data, cross-border data and AI-training-rights clauses for a separate compliance pass.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

选一份 12 页服务合同，按付款/违约/终止/责任上限四项让模型逐条标风险；每条必须带条款号，找不到原文的标「待人工确认」，最后交法务复核。Take a 12-page service contract, have the model flag risks under payment, breach, termination and liability cap; every flag needs a clause number, unsourced ones become “human review”, then route to counsel.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
