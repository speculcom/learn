怎么用 AI 备课How to prepare a lesson with AI

微型指南 · 约 7 分钟Guide · about 7 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

把「找资料」变成「对齐学习目标、设计活动、准备分层材料」的一整套备课流程。Turn “find materials” into a full workflow: align learning goals, design activities and prepare tiered materials.

怎么做How to

先写学习目标（学生上完能做什么）和先备知识，再让 AI 生成内容；目标不明，内容一定泛。

让 AI 给「导入—讲解—练习—总结」四段，每段标时间、师生动作和检查点。

准备三层材料：必做、选做、挑战；AI 很容易只给中间层。

每个事实、例题和图表都回到教材/课标核对，尤其是日期、公式和数据。

让学生知道哪些部分由 AI 生成、哪些经过教师核验；不要把模型输出直接当讲义。

课后用学生错题反推哪一段设计失败，而不是只怪学生不认真。

Write the learning objective (what students can do afterwards) and prerequisites before generating content; vague goals produce vague material.

Ask for four phases — hook, explanation, practice, summary — each with time, teacher/student actions and a checkpoint.

Prepare three tiers: must-do, optional, challenge. AI defaults to the middle tier only.

Check every fact, worked example and chart against the textbook or standard, especially dates, formulas and data.

Tell students which parts are AI-generated and which a teacher verified; do not use model output as a handout.

Use student errors to infer which segment failed, rather than blaming effort alone.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

选一个 45 分钟的知识点，写一条「学生能解释并举例」的目标，让模型出四段活动；你删掉超时环节，补三层练习，并核对每道例题的答案。Pick one 45-minute topic, write an objective (“students can explain and give an example”), have the model draft four phases; cut overtime, add three tiers of practice and verify every worked answer.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
