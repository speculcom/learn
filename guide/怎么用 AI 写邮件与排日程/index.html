怎么用 AI 写邮件与排日程[object Object]

微型指南 · 约 5 分钟Guide · about 5 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

把「写一封邮件」变成「说清目的、给出选项、留好退路」，让 AI 帮你把语气和结构拉平。Turn “write an email” into purpose, options and an exit path; AI smooths tone and structure.

怎么做How to

先写收件人、关系、目的、期望动作和截止时间；缺一项，AI 就会写成一封客气但没用的邮件。

让模型给三个版本：直接版、委婉版、 escalation 版；人选一个再改。

日程请求必须带时区、时长、备选时段和议程；不要只写「方便的时候聊」。

涉及承诺、价格、法律条款的句子，人工逐句确认，不让模型自由发挥。

发送前检查附件、收件人、密送/抄送和敏感信息。

State recipient, relationship, purpose, expected action and deadline; miss one and AI writes a polite but useless email.

Ask for three versions: direct, diplomatic, escalation. Pick one and edit.

Meeting requests need time zone, duration, alternatives and agenda — not “whenever works”.

Confirm every commitment, price or legal sentence by hand; do not let the model improvise.

Before sending, check attachments, recipients, cc/bcc and sensitive information.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

写一封「推迟交付 + 给两个新时间 + 说明补偿」的邮件，让模型出直接/委婉两版；你确认承诺句后，把两个时段写进日历邀请并检查时区。Draft an email that delays delivery, offers two new dates and states compensation; ask for direct and diplomatic versions, confirm every commitment, then put both slots in a calendar invite and check time zones.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
