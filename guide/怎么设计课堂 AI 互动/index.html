怎么设计课堂 AI 互动How to design AI interaction for the classroom

微型指南 · 约 6 分钟Guide · about 6 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

让 AI 做「提问、追问、模拟、即时反馈」，而不是替学生把答案说出来。Let AI ask, probe, simulate and give immediate feedback — not hand students the answer.

怎么做How to

先定互动目标：是激活先备知识、暴露误解，还是练习表达；不同目标用不同 AI 角色。

让 AI 扮演「只会追问的助教」：学生答完先问「你为什么这么想」，不直接判对错。

用匿名/小组提交降低羞耻感；AI 汇总共性问题，教师挑典型讲评。

设置「AI 可能错」的环节，让学生找模型的漏洞，训练批判性判断。

课堂网络、账号、数据与家长授权先确认；不要把学生真实姓名和成绩贴进公共模型。

准备无 AI 的备用方案；网络或工具挂了，课还要能上。

Define the interaction goal: activate prior knowledge, expose misconceptions or practise expression. Each needs a different AI role.

Make AI a “question-only tutor”: after an answer, ask “why do you think so” instead of grading immediately.

Use anonymous or group submissions to lower embarrassment; AI summarises common issues and the teacher picks examples.

Build an “AI can be wrong” segment where students find the model’s flaws, training critical judgement.

Confirm network, accounts, data and parental consent; never paste real student names or grades into a public model.

Prepare a no-AI fallback; the lesson must survive a network or tool failure.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

设计一个 15 分钟活动：AI 只追问「你为什么这么想」，学生分组提交，AI 汇总三类误解，教师挑两类讲评；准备无网络时用的纸质替代。Design a 15-minute activity where AI only asks “why do you think so”; groups submit, AI clusters three misconceptions, the teacher reviews two; prepare a paper fallback for no network.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
