榜单分数高就是好用A High Benchmark Score Means It Works For You

微型指南 · 约 5 分钟Guide · about 5 min · 引用核验 2026-11-03（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

基准只覆盖它测的那几项，与你的场景是两件事。A benchmark covers only what it measures — your task is a different question.

评测分数高的模型，在你的任务上也一定好。A model that scores high on the leaderboards must also be good at your task.

榜单结果受任务集、版本、设置和指标限制。它可以提供初筛证据，但不能替代你的输入、延迟、错误成本与权限条件；不同口径的分数也不能直接比较。Leaderboard results depend on tasks, versions, setup and metrics. They inform initial screening, not your inputs, latency, failure costs or permission constraints. Scores on different bases are not directly comparable.

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

某模型在知识题上领先，但你需要从扫描表格提取金额。先准备相关样本检查识别、格式和漏项，不能直接把知识榜领先写成表格任务更合适。A knowledge-test leader may not suit extracting amounts from scanned tables. Check recognition, formatting and omissions on relevant samples instead of projecting the ranking onto the new task.
