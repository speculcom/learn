怎么用 AI 清洗数据How to clean data with AI

微型指南 · 约 7 分钟Guide · about 7 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

让 AI 帮你发现脏数据、提出清洗规则，但每一步改动都要可回放、可审计。Let AI find dirty data and propose cleaning rules, but every change must be replayable and auditable.

怎么做How to

先做数据剖面：行数、列类型、空值率、重复率、异常值分布；不要直接让模型「清洗一下」。

把清洗规则写成清单：去重键、缺失值策略、日期格式、单位统一、异常值处理。

让 AI 生成可执行的 pandas/SQL 脚本，而不是手改结果表；脚本进版本控制。

每一步保留 before/after 行数与样例，清洗后能回答「删了哪些、为什么删」。

敏感字段先脱敏再给模型看；不要把完整客户表贴进聊天框。

清洗完重跑一遍原始统计，确认没有把信号当噪声删掉。

Profile first: row count, column types, null rate, duplicate rate, outlier distribution. Do not just say “clean it”.

Write the rules as a checklist: dedupe key, missing-value policy, date format, unit normalisation, outlier handling.

Have AI produce executable pandas/SQL, not hand-edited result tables; keep scripts in version control.

Keep before/after row counts and samples for every step; answer “what was deleted and why”.

Mask sensitive fields before sending to a model; never paste a full customer table into chat.

Rerun the original statistics after cleaning to confirm signal was not deleted as noise.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

拿一张有重复、缺失日期和异常金额的表，先做剖面，再让模型写 pandas 脚本；保留 before/after 行数与删除样例，最后重跑总额确认没删掉真实大单。Take a sheet with duplicates, missing dates and outlier amounts; profile it, have the model write pandas code, keep before/after counts and deletion samples, then rerun totals to confirm real large orders were not removed.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
