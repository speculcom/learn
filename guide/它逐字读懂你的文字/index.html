它逐字读懂你的文字It Reads Your Words One By One

微型指南 · 约 5 分钟Guide · about 5 min · 引用核验 2026-11-03（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

它看到的不是字，是词表里的编号。What it sees is not characters but ids from a vocabulary.

它像你一样按字和词理解输入。It reads your input word by word, the way you do.

文本通常先被分成 Token，一个词或字符与一个 Token 不一定一一对应。需要精确计数、拼写或标点检查时，应明确规则。用可核对的方法检查，不要凭聊天表现推断准确性。Text is typically tokenized; tokens and words/characters need not map one-to-one. For exact counting, spelling or punctuation, define rules and use checkable methods rather than inferring accuracy from conversation.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

要统计原文里的某个字符，先规定编码、空格和标点是否计入，再用确定性的计数方法核对。文本会被分成 Token，不能由聊天流畅度推断字符级统计一定准确。For character counts, define encoding and treatment of spaces/punctuation, then verify with deterministic counting. Tokenization and conversational fluency do not establish exact character-counting accuracy.

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
