通用人工智能快到了AGI Is Just Around the Corner

微型指南 · 约 5 分钟Guide · about 5 min · 引用核验 2026-11-03（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

「临近」是说法，不是可核对的里程碑。"Soon" is a claim, not a verifiable milestone.

各家都在说 AGI 临近，说明它有一个可核对的到达时间。Everyone says AGI is near, so there must be a checkable date when it arrives.

AGI 的定义与评价标准并未统一。“临近”只有在能力范围、测试条件和完成标准明确时才可核对；研究目标或时间预测不能直接当作已经实现的证据。AGI definitions and evaluation criteria are not settled. A claim of proximity needs specified capabilities, test conditions and completion criteria; a research goal or forecast does not establish achievement.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

看到“明年达到 AGI”时，先问：AGI 在这里指哪些能力，怎么测，谁复核，日期是承诺还是预测？回答不了这些问题，就把它记作预测，不能当作项目排期的依据。For “AGI next year,” ask which capabilities AGI means, how they are measured, who checks them, and whether the date is a commitment or prediction. Without those answers, record a prediction rather than a planning assumption.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
