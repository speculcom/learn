怎么用 AI 搭 RAG 语料How to build a RAG corpus with AI

微型指南 · 约 8 分钟Guide · about 8 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

RAG 的效果八成取决于语料，而不是模型；先把「收什么、怎么切、怎么测」定下来。RAG quality is mostly corpus quality, not model quality. Decide what to collect, how to chunk and how to test first.

怎么做How to

先定问题域和拒答边界：哪些问题必须答、哪些必须说不知道；语料只收能支撑这些问题的内容。

文档先去噪（页眉页脚、广告、导航），保留标题层级和来源 URL；扫描件先 OCR。

按语义结构切块，不按固定字数硬切；每块带文档标题、章节路径和来源链接。

给每块生成「可检索的问题/摘要」并保留原文，向量检索用问题、生成答案用原文。

建一个小评测集：真实问题 + 标准答案 + 必须引用的文档；每次换切法/模型都重跑。

定期清过期文档，保留版本与删除记录；不要让旧政策和新政策同时被检索到。

Define the question domain and refusal boundary: what must be answered, what must be “I don’t know”; only collect content that supports those.

Denoise documents (headers, footers, ads, navigation), keep heading hierarchy and source URL; OCR scans first.

Chunk by semantic structure, not fixed length; attach document title, section path and source link to each chunk.

Generate searchable questions/summaries per chunk but keep the original text: retrieve with questions, answer with originals.

Build a small eval set: real questions, gold answers, must-cite documents; rerun on every chunker or model change.

Retire stale documents with version and deletion records; never let old and new policies be retrieved together.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

选 20 篇产品文档，去页眉页脚、按章节切块、每块带标题和 URL；用 10 个真实问题测命中与引用，记录哪类问题必须拒答。Take 20 product docs, strip headers/footers, chunk by section with title and URL, then test 10 real questions for hit rate and citation, recording which question types must be refused.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
