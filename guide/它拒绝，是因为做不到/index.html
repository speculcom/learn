它拒绝，是因为做不到Refusal Means Inability

微型指南 · 约 4 分钟Guide · about 4 min · 引用核验 2026-11-03（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

拒答多半是训练出来的选择，不是能力上限。Most refusals are a trained choice, not a capability ceiling.

它说「我不能做这个」，说明它没有这个能力。When it says "I cannot do that", it lacks the capability.

拒答可能与产品政策、权限、输入条件或能力限制有关。先看具体原因和适用规则，不能仅凭一次拒答判断模型有没有某种能力。A refusal may reflect policy, permissions, input conditions or capability limits. Inspect the reason and applicable rules instead of inferring capability from one refusal.

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

一个请求被拒绝，可能涉及权限、产品政策或信息不足。先看提示和适用规则；不要把一次拒绝当成能力测试，也不要为了得到回答而绕过明确的权限限制。A refusal may reflect permissions, product policy or insufficient information. Inspect the message and rules. It is not a standalone capability test or a reason to bypass authorization.
