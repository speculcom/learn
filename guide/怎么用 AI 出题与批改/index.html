怎么用 AI 出题与批改How to generate questions and grade with AI

微型指南 · 约 7 分钟Guide · about 7 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

让 AI 出「能诊断理解」的题，而不是背答案的题；批改给反馈，不只给分数。Generate questions that diagnose understanding, not recall; grade with feedback, not just a score.

怎么做How to

先给知识点、认知层级（记忆/理解/应用/分析）和题型比例，再让 AI 出题。

每题必须带答案、评分点、常见错误和对应知识点；没有评分点的题不要用。

让 AI 生成「干扰项解释」：每个错误选项对应哪种误解，方便讲评。

批改主观题时先给评分 rubric，再让模型按点给分并引用学生原句；人复核边界分。

把 AI 批改结果当作「初评」而不是终评，尤其是作文、开放题和涉及价值的题目。

定期抽查 AI 出题的正确性；模型会编出看似合理但条件矛盾的题。

Specify knowledge points, cognitive level (recall/understanding/application/analysis) and item mix before generating.

Every item needs an answer, scoring points, common errors and mapped knowledge point. Skip items without a rubric.

Ask for distractor explanations: which misconception each wrong option represents.

For open responses, give the rubric first; have the model score by point and quote the student’s sentence; a human checks borderline cases.

Treat AI grading as a first pass, especially essays, open questions and values-related items.

Audit generated items regularly; models invent plausible questions with contradictory conditions.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

让模型出 5 道选择题，每题带答案、评分点、干扰项解释；你挑出 1 道条件矛盾的题，改成可判断的版本，再用 rubric 批 3 份学生答案。Ask for five multiple-choice items with answers, scoring points and distractor explanations; find one contradictory item, rewrite it, then grade three student answers against the rubric.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
