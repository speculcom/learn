写出来的步骤，不保证是产生答案的实际路径。The steps it writes are not guaranteed to be the path that produced the answer.

它给出的推理过程，就是它实际的思考步骤。The reasoning it shows you is the reasoning it actually performed.

生成的中间步骤可以供检查，但不一定完整反映产生答案的内部计算。应核对条件、可验证步骤和结果；解释连贯不能替代这些检查。Generated steps can be checked but need not fully expose the computation producing an answer. Verify conditions, observable steps and results; a coherent explanation is not a substitute.
