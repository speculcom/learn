课堂-选型决策与安全评审Lesson - Selection Decisions and Security Review

课堂教案 · 约 15 分钟Lesson plan · about 15 min · 引用核验 2026-11-07（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-07 (reference links and dates only)

学员把前五个单元的证据汇成一页决策记录，按官方风险框架与提示注入条目做一次评审，写清未验证项与复核日。Learners consolidate evidence from the first five units into a one-page decision record, review it against an official risk framework and prompt-injection guidance, and write unverified items and a review date.

课时目标Lesson objectives

能写出一页决策记录：任务、候选、证据、风险与缓解、未验证项、复核日。

能按模型卡口径核对候选的用途、限制、训练数据与评测条件，缺项写未知。

能把记录按 GOVERN、MAP、MEASURE、MANAGE 四个功能归类，指出哪一栏还空着。

能给出提示注入的缓解动作，含最小权限与高风险动作的人工批准。

Write a one-page decision record: task, candidates, evidence, risks and mitigations, unverified items, review date.

Check candidate use, limits, training data and evaluation conditions in model-card terms, writing unknown for missing items.

Classify the record under GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE, and identify which column is still empty.

Give prompt-injection mitigations including least privilege and human approval for high-risk actions.

步骤Steps
  1. 3 分钟定任务：写成可判定条件，含输入、输出、允许出错范围与复核人角色。
  2. 4 分钟填证据：每个候选只写能回连来源的内容，缺项写未知。
  3. 4 分钟做评审：按四个功能与提示注入条目列风险、缓解动作与责任角色。
  4. 4 分钟写退出：给出退出条件与复核日，小组互评哪一栏最容易被跳过。
  1. 3-minute task definition: write decidable conditions including inputs, outputs, acceptable error range and reviewer role.
  2. 4-minute evidence: for each candidate write only content that links to a source; write unknown for missing items.
  3. 4-minute review: list risks, mitigations and responsible roles under the four functions and the prompt-injection entry.
  4. 4-minute exit: give exit conditions and a review date; groups peer-review which column is most often skipped.
常见误解Common misconceptions

以为评测分数高就可以上线；评测条件与自己的任务条件可能不同。

以为加了护栏就没有风险；权限、日志与人工复核仍在护栏之外。

以为合规框架能替自己做决定；NIST AI RMF 是自愿使用的框架，给结构不给结论。

以为开源或本地部署天然更安全；许可、数据路径与访问控制仍要逐项看。

Assuming a high evaluation score means ready to launch; evaluation conditions may differ from your task conditions.

Assuming guardrails remove risk; permissions, logs and human review still sit outside the guardrail.

Assuming a compliance framework decides for you; NIST AI RMF is a voluntary framework that gives structure, not conclusions.

Assuming open source or local deployment is naturally safer; licence, data path and access control still need item-by-item checks.
评分核对Assessment checks

决策记录六栏齐全，未验证项明确写出，复核日可执行。

每条风险都有缓解动作与验证方式，至少一条涉及权限或人工批准。

能说明这份记录的边界：课堂评审不替代法律意见，也不构成上线批准。

The decision record has all six columns, unverified items are explicit, and the review date is actionable.

Every risk has a mitigation and verification method, with at least one involving permissions or human approval.

Learners can state the record’s boundary: classroom review does not replace legal advice and is not launch approval.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

课堂练习：候选工具评测分数很高，但模型卡缺训练数据与评测条件。决策记录写「未知」，并把最小权限与高风险动作人工批准列为缓解项。Class exercise: a candidate tool has a high evaluation score but its model card lacks training data and evaluation conditions. Write unknown in the decision record and list least privilege plus human approval for high-risk actions as mitigations.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
