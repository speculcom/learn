怎么用 AI 分析表格How to analyse a spreadsheet with AI

微型指南 · 约 7 分钟Guide · about 7 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

先让 AI 理解字段和口径，再让它写公式或查询，最后用原始表复核关键数字。Have AI understand fields and definitions first, then write formulas or queries, then reconcile key numbers against the source sheet.

怎么做How to

先贴表头和 5 行样例，说明每列含义、单位、空值规则；不要让模型猜字段。

把问题拆成「口径 → 计算 → 呈现」：同比还是环比、含不含税、去不去重，先写清楚。

优先让模型生成可检查的公式/SQL，而不是直接给一个结论数字。

关键数字用第二条路径复算（例如透视表 vs 公式），两条路径不一致就以原始表为准。

图表只保留能支持结论的维度；AI 很容易把相关性画成因果。

Show headers and five sample rows; state each column, unit and null rule. Do not let the model guess fields.

Split the question into definition → calculation → presentation: YoY or MoM, tax in or out, deduplicated or not.

Prefer a checkable formula or SQL over a direct number.

Recompute key numbers by a second path (pivot vs formula); if they disagree, the source sheet wins.

Keep only dimensions that support the conclusion; AI easily turns correlation into causation.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

拿一张 500 行的销售表，先写清「含税、去重、按自然月」三个口径，再让模型写透视公式；用第二个公式复算总额，差异超过 0.5% 就回原始表查。Take a 500-row sales sheet, define tax-inclusive, deduplicated and calendar-month rules, then have the model write the pivot formula; recompute totals with a second formula and investigate any gap above 0.5%.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
