怎么判断一张图是不是 AI 生成的How to Tell Whether an Image Is AI-Generated

微型指南 · 约 7 分钟Guide · about 7 min · 引用核验 2026-11-17（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-17 (reference links and dates only)

单看画面已经不可靠；能站住脚的判断来自「来源、原件、凭证、时间线」四条证据链。Looking at pixels is no longer enough. Defensible judgments rest on four evidence chains: source, original file, credentials and timeline.

怎么做How to

先看来源与凭证：C2PA / Content Credentials 能记录拍摄与编辑；没有凭证不等于假，有凭证也要验签。

查反向图搜与发布时间：AI 图常常找不到更早的出处。

看画面线索：文字、手指、重复纹理、镜面反射、阴影方向；但模型进步会不断让这些线索失效。

看噪声与压缩一致性：拼接图常有不同噪声、重复块或边缘不一致。

检测模型只作辅助：它们对压缩、二次编辑和分布外图片很脆弱，概率不是判决。

最可靠的是原件、拍摄链路与证人；平台应保留上传原件和处理日志。

结论分三档：已证实伪造 / 高度可疑 / 无法判断；不要被迫二选一。

Check source and credentials: C2PA / Content Credentials can record capture and edits; no credential does not prove fake, and a credential still needs signature verification.

Run a reverse image search and check publication dates; AI images often have no earlier origin.

Look at visual cues: text, fingers, repeated textures, mirrored reflections and shadow direction — but model progress keeps invalidating them.

Check noise and compression consistency: composites often show mismatched noise, repeated blocks or edge discontinuities.

Use detectors only as aids: they are fragile under compression, re-editing and out-of-distribution images; a probability is not a verdict.

The strongest evidence is the original file, capture chain and witnesses. Platforms should retain the uploaded original and processing logs.

Conclude in three grades: proven fake, highly suspicious, cannot determine. Do not force a binary answer.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

拿到一张图，先记录「来源、原件、凭证、时间线」四项；四项都缺时，结论只能写「无法判断」，不能写「AI 生成」。For any image, record four things first: source, original file, credentials and timeline. If all four are missing, the conclusion is “cannot determine”, not “AI-generated”.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
