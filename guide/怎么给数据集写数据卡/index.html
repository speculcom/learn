怎么给数据集写数据卡[object Object]

微型指南 · 约 6 分钟Guide · about 6 min · 引用核验 2026-11-19（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-19 (reference links and dates only)

数据卡不是简介，而是「来源、授权、构造、偏差、用途边界」的一份可核查档案。A dataset card is not a blurb; it is a checkable record of source, licence, construction, bias and scope.

怎么做How to

先写来源与采集方式：原始出处、时间范围、采集设备/爬取规则、是否含个人数据。

写授权与限制：许可证、商用/再分发/衍生作品条款、地域与期限。

写构造过程：清洗、去重、标注指南、标注者与质检；让别人能复现。

写偏差与覆盖：语言、地域、人群、时间分布；明确哪些群体/场景缺失。

写用途边界：适合训练/评测/检索的哪一类，不适合什么；给出已知失败案例。

给版本、哈希、更新记录和联系人；数据卡不是一次写完就结束。

Write source and collection method: origin, time range, device/crawl rules, whether personal data is included.

Write licence and limits: commercial use, redistribution, derivatives, territory and term.

Write construction: cleaning, dedupe, annotation guide, annotators and QA, enough for reproduction.

Write bias and coverage: language, geography, population, time; state what is missing.

Write scope: which training/eval/retrieval use it fits, what it does not, plus known failure cases.

Include version, hash, changelog and contact; a dataset card is not write-once.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

选一个 1 万条的评价数据集，补来源、许可证、清洗与标注、偏差与用途边界五节；每节至少一条可点击出处，最后写一条已知失败案例。Take a 10k-row review dataset and fill source, licence, cleaning/annotation, bias and scope sections; each needs a clickable source, plus one known failure case.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
