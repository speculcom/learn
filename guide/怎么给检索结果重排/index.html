怎么给检索结果重排How to Rerank Retrieval Results

微型指南 · 约 7 分钟Guide · about 7 min · 引用核验 2026-11-03（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

检索先取回候选，重排再调整顺序。它能否改善最终结果，要看候选是否包含答案、排序质量和延迟预算。Retrieval returns candidates; reranking changes their order. Benefits depend on answer coverage, ranking quality and latency budget.

怎么做How to

粗筛多取：向量检索先取回几十条候选，别只取前三。

精筛换模型：重排模型逐条比对查询与候选，比向量距离更细。

重排有成本：候选越多越慢越贵，按延迟预算定候选数。

看重排模型的语种支持：不是所有重排模型都支持中文。

调 top-k：最终喂给模型的条数宁少勿滥，无关块会带偏回答。

评测两阶段分别量：粗筛召回率和精筛排序分开看，才知道哪阶段拖后腿。

Coarse pass retrieves wide: pull dozens of candidates, not just the top three.

Fine pass switches models: a reranker compares query and candidate item by item, finer than vector distance.

Reranking has a cost: more candidates means slower and pricier; set the candidate count by your latency budget.

Check language support of the reranker: not every reranking model handles Chinese.

Tune top-k: fewer final items beats more — irrelevant chunks drag the answer off course.

Measure the two stages separately: coarse recall and fine ranking are scored apart, so you know which stage drags.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

问题是“2025 版报销上限”。候选里同时有 2023、2025 版和讨论帖。先确认正确版本已被召回，再看重排是否把它放到前面；正确文档根本没进候选时，换重排器不能补回它。For “2025 reimbursement limit,” candidates include 2023 and 2025 policies plus discussion posts. Confirm that the correct version was retrieved before assessing its rank. A reranker cannot restore an absent candidate.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
