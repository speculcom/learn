怎么评测 RAG 的效果How to Evaluate a RAG System

微型指南 · 约 8 分钟Guide · about 8 min · 引用核验 2026-11-03（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-03 (reference links and dates only)

RAG 出问题可能出在检索，也可能出在生成。评测要分开量，不然修错地方。A RAG failure can come from retrieval or from generation. Evaluate them separately, or you will fix the wrong part.

怎么做How to

先备评测集：真实问题加标准答案，几十条就够起步。

检索侧量召回：该找到的块有没有进候选，这是第一道关。

生成侧量忠实度：回答里的每句话能不能被检索到的块支撑。

答案相关性单独量：答得对题但依据错，也是问题。

用 RAGAS 这类框架自动评，人工抽查校准它的判分。

分场景看分数：不同问题类型分开统计，平均分掩盖短板。

Build an eval set first: real questions with gold answers; a few dozen is enough to start.

Retrieval side, measure recall: did the chunk that should be found make it into the candidates?

Generation side, measure faithfulness: can every sentence in the answer be supported by the retrieved chunks?

Measure answer relevance separately: on-topic answers with wrong grounding are still a problem.

Automate with a framework like RAGAS, then spot-check by hand to calibrate its scoring.

Break scores down by scenario: average scores hide the weak spots of specific question types.

例子与判断Example and interpretation

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

问题问“报销上限”，正确文档已取回，回答却引用了餐费额度。这是生成或证据使用错误。若取回的全是旧版政策，则先修检索。把这两类失败分别记账，才知道该改哪一段。If the right reimbursement policy was retrieved but the answer uses a meal allowance, inspect generation/evidence use. If every retrieved document is outdated, inspect retrieval first. Record the two failure types separately.

做完例子后怎么检查How to check your practice

先用自己的话解释上面的判断，再检查输入条件，最后回原文验证。说不清原因时回概念；缺日期、字段或权限时补输入；结论无出处时先撤回结论。Explain the example in your own words, inspect input constraints, then verify against the source. Revisit concepts if you cannot explain why; supply missing dates, fields or permissions; withdraw unsupported conclusions.
