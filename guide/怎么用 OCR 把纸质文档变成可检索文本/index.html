怎么用 OCR 把纸质文档变成可检索文本How to Turn Paper Documents into Searchable Text

微型指南 · 约 9 分钟Guide · about 9 min · 引用核验 2026-11-17（仅引用链接与日期） · refs checked 2026-11-17 (reference links and dates only)

扫描件不是文本；OCR 只是第一步，真正决定检索质量的是版面还原、校对与分块。A scan is not text. OCR is only the first step; layout recovery, proofreading and chunking decide whether retrieval works.

怎么做How to

先看来源：原生 PDF 有文本层就先抽取；只有扫描件或照片才走 OCR。

预处理：去畸变、去噪、纠偏、裁边；手机拍摄先做透视校正。

版面分析：先分栏、表格、页眉页脚，别把两栏读成一行、把表格拍平成文本。

逐页留置信度：低置信区域标出来人工复核，不要整本信任。

校对关键字段：金额、日期、编号、专有名词；这些错最贵。

分块与索引：按标题层级切，保留页码与来源，再接入 RAG。

验收：抽 30 页算字符错误率、表格行列还原率、关键字段准确率，达标再上线。

Check the source first: a born-digital PDF already has a text layer; only scans and photos need OCR.

Preprocess: deskew, denoise and crop; correct perspective on phone photos.

Analyse layout: separate columns, tables, headers and footers; never read two columns as one line or flatten a table.

Keep per-page confidence: flag low-confidence regions for human review instead of trusting the whole book.

Proofread key fields: amounts, dates, identifiers and proper nouns — these errors cost the most.

Chunk and index by heading hierarchy, keeping page numbers and source metadata, then feed RAG.

Acceptance: sample 30 pages, measure character error rate, table-structure accuracy and key-field accuracy before launch.

以下是教学情境，不是本站运行或实测记录。A teaching scenario, not a measured or executed site test.

先拿 30 页真实样本跑一遍：记录字符错误率、表格行列还原率和关键字段准确率。三项达标之前，不要把这批 OCR 结果接进检索库。Run 30 real pages first and record character error rate, table structure accuracy and key-field accuracy. Do not connect the OCR output to a retrieval index until all three pass.
